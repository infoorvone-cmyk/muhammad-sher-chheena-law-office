A Quarter-Century of Judicial Excellence

With more than 25 years of continuous standing before the honorable judges of the Punjab High Court and the Supreme Court of Pakistan, Advocate Muhammad Sher Chheena has built a reputation grounded in rigorous statutory preparation, forensic scrutiny of trial evidence, and fearless oral advocacy.

His legal practice spans all 254 judicial districts across Pakistan, having represented individuals, prominent commercial enterprises, and expatriate Pakistanis in high-stakes civil land disputes, contested inheritance partitions, white-collar financial investigations, and fundamental constitutional writ petitions.

Trial & Appellate Philosophy

In Pakistan's complex judicial system, litigation is frequently weaponized through delayed procedures, fraudulent revenue entries, and fictitious police FIRs. Advocate Muhammad Sher Chheena operates on a strict policy of transparent legal advice:

  • Direct Senior Counsel Oversight: Every case file, revenue fard, and court petition is personally drafted, scrutinized, and argued by Senior Advocate Muhammad Sher Chheena without delegation to junior apprentices.
  • Uncompromising Merit Assessment: If a case lacks legal foundation or appeals chances are slim, clients receive direct, candid counsel rather than false assurances designed to extract prolonged legal retainers.
  • Expedited Injunctive Relief: Specializing in securing urgent ad-interim stay orders from High Court benches to prevent illegal land transfers, unauthorized construction, and malicious arrests before irreversible damage occurs.

The Overseas Pakistani Mission

Having witnessed countless overseas families lose ancestral lands to local land-grabbing syndicates (Qabza mafias) while residing abroad in the UK, United States, Canada, and the Gulf, Advocate Muhammad Sher Chheena instituted the Overseas Pakistani Legal Cell.

This dedicated cell ensures that expatriates can protect, recover, and manage their Pakistani properties through digitally verified Special Power of Attorney (PoA) and scheduled virtual video consultations, without requiring the client to undertake costly and disruptive emergency travel to Pakistan.
