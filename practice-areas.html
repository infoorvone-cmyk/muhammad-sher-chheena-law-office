Appellate & Trial Jurisdictions

Decisive courtroom defense across any civil or criminal matter before trial courts, Punjab High Court, and the Supreme Court of Pakistan.

1. Civil Litigation & Land Laws

Civil land litigation in Pakistan is notoriously protracted and fraught with bureaucratic tampering. Advocate Muhammad Sher Chheena has secured hundreds of landmark judgments reversing fraudulent property transfers, canceling illegal revenue entries (fard/inteqal), and enforcing contracts:

  • Declaration of Title (Dawa Istiqrar-e-Haq): Establishing absolute legal ownership over disputed residential plots, commercial plazas, and agricultural estates.
  • Specific Performance of Contract (Tameel-e-Mukhtas): Enforcing registered agreements to sell against dishonest sellers who attempt to renege after receiving token deposits.
  • Permanent & Temporary Injunctions (Stay Orders): Obtaining urgent interim stay orders under Order XXXIX Rules 1 & 2 CPC to prevent property demolition, sale, or alienation.
  • Cancellation of Fraudulent Deeds (Mansookhi Dastawaiz): Striking down forged registry deeds, fake powers of attorney, and bogus gift transfers (Hiba).
Civil Courts • Senior Civil Judges • Punjab High Court

2. Supreme Court & High Court Constitutional Writs

When state authorities, police departments, regulatory bodies, or administrative tribunals act without lawful jurisdiction, constitutional writ petitions provide rapid, binding redress:

  • Writ of Mandamus & Prohibition (Article 199): Compelling government bodies, LDA, Revenue departments, and law enforcement agencies to discharge their statutory duties.
  • Writ of Certiorari: Quashing arbitrary administrative orders, illegal notifications, and decisions passed in violation of natural justice.
  • Supreme Court Appeals (Civil & Criminal Petitions for Leave to Appeal - CPLA): Crafting sophisticated appellate grounds challenging flawed High Court judgments before the Supreme Court of Pakistan.
  • Intra-Court Appeals (ICA) & Review Petitions: Challenging single-bench High Court orders before a division bench.
Punjab High Court • Supreme Court of Pakistan

3. Criminal Defense, Bail & Special Statutes

Vigorous, ethical defense in criminal proceedings where liberty and reputation are on the line. We provide strategic counsel from the initial police investigation through trial and high court appeals:

  • Pre-Arrest Bail (Zamanat Qabal-az-Giraftari): Shielding clients from malicious or politically motivated arrests through urgent High Court and Sessions Court protective bail orders.
  • Post-Arrest Bail: Securing release on bail in non-bailable offenses under Section 497 CrPC.
  • FIR Quashment (Section 561-A CrPC): High Court writ petitions to quash frivolous and false criminal cases instituted to harass clients.
  • Trial Advocacy: Defense representation in murder trials (PPC 302), cybercrime (PECA), National Accountability Bureau (NAB), and Federal Investigation Agency (FIA) matters.
Sessions Court • Special Anti-Corruption Courts • High Court

4. Inheritance, Succession & Trust Administration

Settling complex ancestral estates, resolving multi-heir distribution disputes, and protecting overseas heirs:

  • Succession Certificates & Letters of Administration: Rapid court decrees for transferring bank accounts, family businesses, and immovable assets.
  • Partition Suits (Dawa Taqseem): Physical partition of jointly-owned agricultural land and commercial buildings through court-appointed revenue commissions.
  • Protection of Women’s & Overseas Heirs' Inheritance Rights: Enforcement of statutory safeguards against relatives seeking to deprive female or foreign heirs of their Shariah inheritance.
Civil Courts • Revenue Courts • High Court
