Overseas Pakistani Legal Protection Cell

Decisive courtroom defense, asset recovery, and remote power of attorney representation for expatriates worldwide.

Defending Your Homeland Assets From Abroad

Overseas Pakistanis remit over $30 billion annually, yet their properties, agricultural acreage, and commercial plots remain the prime targets of unscrupulous relatives, tenants refusing eviction, and organized land-grabbing cartels (Qabza mafias).

When a legal emergency strikes in Lahore, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Gujranwala, or rural Punjab, expatriates face crippling obstacles: exorbitant emergency flights, corrupt local middlemen, delayed hearings, and physical intimidation. Advocate Muhammad Sher Chheena's Overseas Legal Cell was founded to resolve these disputes through direct, accountable, senior Supreme Court representation.

Core Overseas Legal Services

🛡️ Property Qabza & Illegal Dispossession Recovery

Filing prompt criminal petitions under the Illegal Dispossession Act 2005 before the Sessions Court and seeking urgent writ remedies from the High Court. We obtain warrants for police-assisted physical repossession of homes, plazas, and agricultural land unlawfully occupied during the owner's absence abroad.

High Court Injunction • Sessions Court

📜 Ancestral Wirasat, Succession & Partition Suits

Securing official Succession Certificates and Letters of Administration from competent courts to release bank accounts, shares, and real estate. In contested family estates, we institute Partition Suits (Dawa Taqseem-e-Zameen) to carve out your lawful Shariah and statutory share with court-appointed local commissioners.

Civil Court • High Court Appeals

🔏 Power of Attorney (PoA) Drafting & Attestation

Drafting bulletproof Special or General Power of Attorney documents that strictly limit an agent's authority to litigation defense without permitting unauthorized sale. We guide clients through the complete Pakistani High Commission / Embassy attestation procedure in London, New York, Dubai, or Riyadh, followed by Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) attestation and District Registrar registration in Pakistan.

MOFA Compliant • Zero Travel Required

🔍 Pre-Purchase Revenue & Registry Record Audits

Before investing millions into housing societies or private land, our chambers conduct an exhaustive forensic title search. We examine the Aks Shajra, Jamabandi, Fard Malkiat, Khasra Girdawari, and verify whether the property has any pending court stay orders, bank mortgages, or bogus duplicate registry deeds.

Tehsil • Patwari • Sub-Registrar Checks

How Remote Representation Works (4-Step Process)

  1. 1. Initial $200 Virtual Video Consultation: Schedule a 60-minute Zoom or WhatsApp video conference directly with Advocate Muhammad Sher Chheena to assess the merits, legal remedies, and stay order viability.
  2. 2. Digital File Intake & Document Review: Submit scanned copies of registry deeds, fard, FIRs, or court notices via secure encrypted email or WhatsApp for scrutiny.
  3. 3. Power of Attorney Execution: Sign the tailored Vakalatnama or Special Power of Attorney through your local Pakistani consulate/embassy.
  4. 4. Court Action & Regular Reporting: Senior Advocate Muhammad Sher Chheena files the petition, appears before High Court/Supreme Court benches, and provides certified court orders and progress briefs directly to you.
