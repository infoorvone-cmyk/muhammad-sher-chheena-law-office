Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the consultation fee fixed at $200/hr for overseas clients?
To ensure serious, focused legal analysis without bureaucratic delays. The 60-minute session is conducted directly by Senior Advocate Muhammad Sher Chheena (not junior associates) and includes preliminary statutory review, court jurisdiction clarification, and actionable legal guidance tailored to foreign time zones.
How do I pay for the consultation from the UK, USA, or Gulf?
Overseas clients can remit via international bank wire, major credit cards, or authorized digital remittance channels (Remitly, Wise, Western Union) with an official chamber receipt issued upon confirmation.
What documents should I prepare before our consultation?
Please prepare scanned copies or clear photos of all relevant registry documents (Bainama), Fard Malkiat, Aks Shajra, FIR copies, lower court decrees, or eviction notices. These can be shared directly via WhatsApp or encrypted email prior to the call.
Is our consultation protected by attorney-client privilege?
Yes, 100%. All discussions, documents shared, and tactical options explored remain strictly confidential under Pakistani legal ethics (Legal Practitioners and Bar Councils Act 1973) and universal attorney-client privilege rules.
